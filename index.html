A little truth

Some feelings don't need to be loud.

“I’m still waiting for you — not because I expect anything, but because my heart hasn't learned how to stop caring.”

I don't need your whole day. Even a few minutes with you can make an ordinary day feel different. I just miss being able to talk to you, laugh with you, and share little moments that somehow became important to me.

I know I can't make you feel something by asking for it. I don't want my feelings to become pressure. I just want you to know that they are genuine — and that meeting you again would mean a lot to me.

So this page isn't a demand. It's simply me putting my heart somewhere you can see it.
